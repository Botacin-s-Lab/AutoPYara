CLAIM 8 - Paper claim 5: threat hunting on held-out samples
================================================================================

CLAIM (paper claim 5, figures in Claim5_Threathunting/)
    In the threat-hunting experiment each cluster is split into training samples,
    from which the rule is generated, and held-out test samples (the Ratio_0.75
    split). On the held-out samples AutoPYara's rules keep a higher TP rate than
    AutoYara's (mean of the TP curve, sdhash clusterings, thresholds 50-90 pooled):

                                        train            test (held out)
                                  AutoPYara AutoYara   AutoPYara AutoYara
        with heuristics only        81.9 %   69.8 %      80.8 %   69.4 %
        without heuristics          80.4 %   75.0 %      76.3 %   75.8 %
        real-world stream           84.8 %   76.2 %      82.2 %   75.9 %

    (Claim wording derived from the figure names and the plotted values; the
    numbers are what the provided data produce.)

    3. The claim holds: in all three experiments AutoPYara's held-out (test) curve
       mean is higher than AutoYara's.

    "CLAIM 8: PASS". Reference figures: expected/figures/. Transcript:

NOTE ON MARGINS
    With heuristics only, AutoPYara leads by about 11 points on held-out samples.
    Without heuristics the lead is small (about 0.5 points), so that part of the
    claim rests on the direction, not on a large margin. The real-world stream
    leads by about 6.3 points.
