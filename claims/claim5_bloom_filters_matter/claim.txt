CLAIM 5 - Paper claim 2: the Bloom filters matter
================================================================================

CLAIM (paper claim 2, figures in Claim2_BloomFiltersMatter/)
    Retraining the benign/malicious Bloom filters on the target data, instead of
    using AutoYara's original Ember-trained filters, raises the mean TP rate of
    both tools, dramatically so on the SSdeep and J-SDhash clusterings:

                          Ember filters   retrained filters
        AutoYara   SSdeep     11.2 %          92.7 %
                   J-SDhash   29.4 %          92.1 %
                   VirusTotal 33.6 %          36.4 %
        AutoPYara  SSdeep     10.9 %          93.7 %
                   J-SDhash   29.8 %          92.8 %
                   VirusTotal 40.2 %          44.8 %

    (Claim wording derived from the figure names and the plotted values; the
    numbers are what the provided data produce.)

    Claim2_BloomFiltersMatter/AutoPYara_EmberBloomFiltersVSRetrainedBloomfilters.pdf
        light bars = retrained filters, dark bars = Ember filters

    Each bar is the mean TP rate over all clusters (pooled over the thresholds),

INPUTS

HOW TO RUN
    ./run.sh [-j N]

    Prerequisites: ../../install.sh and the evaluation data in ../../data.
    Runtime: about 10 s on 8 cores.

WHAT IS VERIFIED (artifact/plots/verify_claims.py)
    1. Both figures are regenerated from the data.
    2. Every plotted number matches expected/values.json within 1.0 percentage point.
    3. The claim holds: for both tools and all three clusterings the retrained
       filters give a higher mean TP rate than the Ember filters, and on SSdeep and
       J-SDhash the gain is at least 50 percentage points.
