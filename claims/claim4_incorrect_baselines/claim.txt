CLAIM 4 - Paper claim 1: AutoYara's baseline numbers hide the clusters it fails on
================================================================================

CLAIM (paper claim 1, figures in Claim1_IncorrectBaseLines/)
    With its original (Ember) Bloom filters, AutoYara looks accurate only if the
    clusters it fails on are left out. Averaged over the clusters where its rule
    reached a non-zero TP rate, its mean TP rate is 87.0 % (SSdeep clusters),
    90.5 % (J-SDhash) and 67.7 % (VirusTotal). Averaged over ALL clusters, which is
    the correct baseline, it is only 11.2 %, 29.4 % and 33.6 %.

    (Claim wording derived from the figure names and the plotted values; the
    numbers are what the provided data produce.)

FIGURES
    Claim1_IncorrectBaseLines/AutoYara_baselineBoxPlotEMBF.pdf   zero-TP clusters excluded
    Claim1_IncorrectBaseLines/AutoYara_EMBF.pdf                  all clusters

    Each bar is the mean TP rate of all clusters, pooled over the five similarity
    thresholds (50-90) of the SSdeep and J-SDhash clusterings, and over the single
    VirusTotal-label clustering. Error bars are the standard deviation.

INPUTS
    data/clusterCSV/{ssdeep,sdhash,virusTotal}/...
    data/ruleEval/originalBloomFilters/{ssdeep/AutoYaraBaseline,sdhash/AutoYara,
                                        virusTotal/AutoyaraBaseline}/...
    Every rule carries the TP rate it achieved on its cluster ("// Input TP Rate: X/Y").

HOW TO RUN
    ./run.sh [-j N]          (N worker processes; default: all CPUs)

    Prerequisites: ../../install.sh has been run and the evaluation data is in
    ../../data (artifact/download_data.py). Runtime: about 10 s on 8 cores,
    under 1 min on 1 core.

WHAT IS VERIFIED (artifact/plots/verify_claims.py)
    1. Both figures are regenerated from the data.
    2. Every plotted number (bar heights and error bars) matches the reference
       values in expected/values.json within 1.0 percentage point.
    3. The claim holds on the regenerated numbers: for every clustering, the mean
       over all clusters is at least 30 percentage points below the mean over the
       non-zero clusters.

EXPECTED RESULT
    "CLAIM 4: PASS". The reference figures are in expected/figures/ and the full
    transcript is in expected/expected_output.txt.

TOLERANCE
    The figures are a deterministic function of the provided data, so a correct
    run matches the reference exactly (deviation 0.00). The 1.0-point tolerance
    only absorbs differences between numeric-library versions.

SCOPE
    The rule-generation runs that produced the evaluated rules need the paper's
    malware corpus, which cannot be redistributed. As agreed with the AEC, the
    artifact ships the generated rules with their measured TP rates, and this
    claim regenerates the paper's figure and numbers from them.
